The 18 source durations total about 1 minute 56 seconds. They are treated as short improvised clips rather than standalone songs: playback chooses a shuffled order, avoids an immediate repeat at bag boundaries, and crossfades between clips. The existing user-recorded battle theme remains the combat pool. Dungeon events and visitors keep the dungeon pool; no recordings were assigned to town or separate event stingers.

The supplied batch was described by the user as dungeon sounds. This audit could measure signal properties but did not establish a trustworthy per-file musical/rhythmic description or subjective loop rating, so no finer individual musical labels are claimed. All clips remain grouped only under the requested dungeon context.
